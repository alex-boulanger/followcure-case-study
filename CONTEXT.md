# FollowCure

FollowCure supports practitioner-led follow-up for patients whose supplement course may be nearing its end.

## Language

**Practitioner**:
Simplycure's customer, who prescribes a protocol and manages the patient relationship.

**Patient**:
The person receiving a practitioner's recommendation and deciding whether to place an order.

**Protocol**:
The practitioner's recommended supplements and intended course duration. A recommendation does not establish that the patient purchased or started the course.

**Course**:
A period during which a patient takes the supplements in a protocol. Its estimated end does not establish a need to continue.

**Order**:
A patient's purchase of supplements. An order may fulfill a protocol or represent a later purchase.

**Refill**:
A repeat purchase intended to continue the same course or protocol. An unrelated later order is not a refill.

**Follow-up**:
A practitioner-led decision about the next step for a particular patient and course cycle, which may lead to a refill, an appointment, both, or no further action.

**GMV**:
The value of orders placed through the platform. Simplycure earns a margin on those sales; GMV is distinct from its revenue and profit.
